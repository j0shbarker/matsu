# Matsu privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Matsu is an unofficial study app for Japanese: a client for your WaniKani account plus
"Gap Vocab", extra vocabulary practice. Matsu is not affiliated with or endorsed by WaniKani or
Tofugu.

**In short: Matsu doesn't collect any data about you. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone.**

## What Matsu stores, and where

Everything is stored **only on your device**:

- **Your Gap Vocab progress**: lessons, review history, stages and settings.
- **Your practice test scores** (the Tests tab): the date, level and score of each test you take, and
  your test settings. They are practice only and are never sent anywhere.
- **A copy of your WaniKani data**: your level, items, progress, review times, synonyms and
  notes, downloaded from WaniKani so the app works offline. Matsu refreshes it when you open the app
  and now and then in the background (iOS decides when). Reviews you do offline are kept on the
  device until they've been sent to WaniKani.
- **Offline audio** (if you turn it on): WaniKani's pronunciation recordings, saved on the device.
- **Widget and reminder data**: counts and due times, shared only with Matsu's own widget.

Sentences in the listening tests are read aloud by the voices built into iOS, on the device.

There is no Matsu account and no Matsu server. Matsu doesn't sync your data to iCloud or anywhere
else. We (the developer) never receive any of it.

## Your WaniKani API token

To use the WaniKani side, you give Matsu a personal access token that you make on
wanikani.com. Matsu:

- stores the token in the iPhone's **Keychain**, on this device only;
- sends it **only to WaniKani** (api.wanikani.com), to read your data and, if you allow it, to
  save your lessons, reviews, synonyms and notes;
- never asks for or sees your WaniKani password.

You choose what the token may do when you make it. With a read-only token, Matsu never changes
anything on your WaniKani account. You can disconnect at any time (WaniKani settings ‚Üí
Disconnect), and you can delete the token on wanikani.com.

## Who Matsu talks to

- **WaniKani** (wanikani.com and the addresses WaniKani gives for its images and audio), with your
  token, as described above. WaniKani's own privacy policy applies to your WaniKani account.
- **Nobody else.** Matsu contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and
  doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Some item pages have a "Jisho" link and the settings have links to wanikani.com and licence pages.
They open in Safari when you tap them, like any link, and Matsu sends nothing along with them
except the word you looked up on Jisho.

Crash reports, if you've chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings, come through
Apple and contain no personal data from Matsu.

## Notifications

Review reminders are **local notifications** planned on your iPhone from your own due times. No
push service or server is involved. You can turn them off in Matsu's settings or in iOS Settings.

## Backups

**Export a backup** (Gap Vocab Settings) makes a single file with your Gap Vocab progress, review
history, test scores and settings. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email‚Ä¶) is your choice,
and it's then handled by the service you chose. The backup doesn't contain your WaniKani token or
your WaniKani data (that stays on WaniKani). **Restore from a backup** reads such a file back in.

## Deleting your data

- **Gap Vocab Settings ‚Üí Delete all data** removes everything Matsu keeps on the device: Gap Vocab
  progress and history, test scores, your WaniKani token and the copy of your WaniKani data, offline
  audio, widget data, reminders and settings.
- Deleting the app also removes all of this.
- Your WaniKani account itself isn't affected. To delete it, see WaniKani's website.
- Backup files you've exported are yours; delete them wherever you saved them.

## Children

Matsu doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. WaniKani sets its own
age rules for its accounts.
